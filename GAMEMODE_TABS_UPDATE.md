# ONYX Gamemode Tabs

The leaderboard now follows the current MCTiers-style gamemode navigation, with LTMs intentionally removed.

Tabs:
- Overall
- Vanilla
- UHC
- Pot
- NethOP
- SMP
- Sword
- Axe
- Mace

Selecting a gamemode filters and sorts the ONYX leaderboard for that gamemode.
On the Overall tab, all tested kit badges remain visible.
Hovering a kit badge displays that player's points for that gamemode.

ONYX tier points:
LT5 1, HT5 2, LT4 3, HT4 4, LT3 6, HT3 10, LT2 20, HT2 30, LT1 45, HT1 60.
